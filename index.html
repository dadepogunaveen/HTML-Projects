Document Once upon a time, there lived a poor woodcutter in a small village.
Every day, he went to the forest to cut wood. One day,
while cutting a tree near a river, his axe fell into the water.
He became very sad and started crying. Suddenly,
a fairy appeared and asked him why he was crying.
She brought a golden axe and asked if it belonged to him.
The woodcutter said no because he wanted only his own axe.
The fairy was happy with his honesty and gave him the golden and silver axes as a reward.
The woodcutter thanked the fairy and returned home happily. From that day, he lived a better life.
